Funny Chess

Chess for kids 6+ against friendly robots. Free, in the browser, no sign-up.

▶ Play free on itch.io

What's inside

  • Three robots: Newbie, Trickster and Pro. They think out loud, react to your moves and never laugh at mistakes. Tap the robot and it moves at once.
  • Nine worlds: Puzzle, Classic, Notebook, Chocolate, Clouds, Space, Junkyard, Islands and City. Switch at any time.
  • Hints that teach: dots show where a piece can go, lines show how the knight jumps.
  • Take back a move, even after checkmate.
  • See why it is checkmate: arrows show who gives check and which piece keeps the king off each square.
  • Coins for moves, captures, checks, castling and promotion.
  • Several languages. Made for phones and tablets held upright.

For parents and teachers

  • Age 6 and up. Kids who don't know the rules yet learn them by tapping pieces.
  • No ads, no accounts, no data collection.
  • Best on a tablet held upright.

Who made it

A dad and his daughter made Funny Chess together, and her younger brother tested it and asked for more. It is our first game.

Ideas for later: a "no hints" mode, sound, playing as black, something to spend coins on.
